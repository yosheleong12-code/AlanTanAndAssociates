About the firm

Since 2012

Founded in Muar

Founded in Muar in 2012 by Managing Partner Alan M. L. Tan as a sole-practitioner firm, Alan Tan & Associates has grown steadily alongside the clients and communities we serve. Today, we operate from three offices across Johor—Muar, Batu Pahat and Johor Bahru—while undertaking matters throughout Peninsular Malaysia.

Our growth is guided by a simple belief: legal solutions should never be one size fits all. Every client, circumstance and challenge is different. We therefore take the time to understand what matters, tailor our approach and deliver legal services that are practical, effective and responsive to each client’s needs.

Behind the firm is an agile and committed team of lawyers and support personnel. We are trusted with demanding disputes, challenging cases and complex transactions, approaching each matter with care, determination and close attention to detail. We continue to adapt, evolve and improve the way we work so that our clients receive efficient and effective legal support.

We also believe that access to justice should not depend solely on a person’s means. As part of our commitment to the wider community, we provide pro bono legal assistance to deserving individuals who may otherwise be unable to obtain representation.

From a sole-practitioner practice to a growing multi-office firm, our purpose remains unchanged: to provide professional legal services shaped around the people we serve—One Firm, Tailored Solutions.
